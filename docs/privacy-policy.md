# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 July 2026**

Vida Verde Centro de Español ("Vida Verde", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal data when you:

- visit our website or customer portal;
- contact us by form, email, telephone, or WhatsApp;
- download a guide or subscribe to our emails;
- book or attend an online Spanish lesson;
- purchase a class or class package;
- enquire about or join an in-person programme, homestay, cultural activity, or travel-Spanish service; or
- otherwise interact with Vida Verde.

We process personal data in accordance with Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales, or "LOPDP"), its regulations, and other laws that apply to us. Where the law of another country applies to a particular person or service, including the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation, we will also respect the rights and obligations required by that law.

## 2. Personal data we collect

The personal data we collect depends on how you use our services.

### Information you give us

This may include:

- your name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, country, preferred language, and timezone;
- account credentials and account-recovery information;
- your Spanish level, learning goals, preferred teacher, availability, lesson history, attendance, homework, progress, feedback, and communications with teachers or our team;
- booking and purchase details, such as the service or package selected, date and time, amount, currency, payment status, receipt, invoice, and transaction reference;
- enquiry details, including the programme, homestay, or travel service that interests you and the message you send us;
- for an in-person programme or homestay, information reasonably needed to organise your stay, such as travel dates, itinerary, passport or identity details where required, emergency contact, accommodation preferences, dietary needs, accessibility needs, and relevant health or safety information;
- marketing preferences and records of consent, subscription, or unsubscribe requests;
- survey responses, reviews, testimonials, photographs, video, or other content you choose to provide; and
- any other information you voluntarily include in a form, email, WhatsApp message, or conversation with us.

Please do not send sensitive health, identity, or travel information unless we request it and it is necessary to arrange or safely deliver a service.

### Information collected automatically

When you use our website or portal, we may collect:

- IP address, device type, browser, operating system, language, and approximate location;
- pages viewed, links and buttons clicked, booking-form activity, referral source, date and time of access, and similar usage information;
- cookie identifiers, session identifiers, language preference, and consent choices; and
- security and diagnostic information, such as login attempts, error logs, and fraud indicators.

We use non-essential analytics or advertising cookies only where permitted and, when required, after obtaining your consent. See Section 9.

### Information received from other sources

We may receive information from:

- payment processors, such as confirmation that a payment succeeded, failed, or was refunded. We do not receive or store your complete card number or card security code;
- booking and calendar providers;
- Google Meet and other tools used to deliver a lesson;
- email, hosting, analytics, mapping, and customer-support providers;
- a parent, guardian, employer, educational institution, travel organiser, or another person making an authorised booking for you;
- teachers, host families, transport providers, or activity partners involved in delivering your service; and
- social media or review platforms when you contact us or interact with our public pages.

If someone provides personal data about another person, they must be authorised to do so and must make this Policy available to that person.

## 3. Why we use personal data and our legal bases

We use personal data only when we have a lawful reason.

### To respond and take steps before a contract

We use contact, learning, travel, and preference information to answer questions, recommend a suitable teacher or programme, prepare a quote, check availability, and help you complete a booking.

### To provide and manage contracted services

We use personal data to:

- create and manage your account;
- process bookings, payments, receipts, invoices, credits, rescheduling, cancellations, and refunds;
- schedule lessons and send confirmations, reminders, calendar invitations, Google Meet links, preparation material, and follow-up messages;
- deliver classes, maintain learning records, and recommend suitable packages;
- organise in-person classes, homestays, airport transfers, cultural activities, or travel-Spanish arrangements;
- provide customer support and resolve complaints; and
- communicate important operational changes.

### With your consent

Where consent is the appropriate legal basis, we may use personal data to:

- send newsletters, Spanish-learning tips, offers, and nurture emails;
- place non-essential cookies or run optional analytics;
- publish a testimonial, photograph, recording, or student story;
- process optional health, dietary, accessibility, or other sensitive information; or
- use data for another purpose clearly explained when consent is requested.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not make earlier processing unlawful and may mean we cannot provide an optional feature or a service that depends on the information.

### For our legitimate interests

Where permitted, we may process data as reasonably necessary to:

- secure our website, portal, accounts, staff, students, and services;
- prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorised access;
- keep appropriate business records;
- understand how our services are used and improve them;
- manage teacher availability and service quality; and
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Before relying on legitimate interests, we consider the purpose, necessity, and effect on your rights. You may object to this processing as described in Section 12.

### To comply with law

We may process and retain information to comply with tax, accounting, consumer-protection, safeguarding, data-protection, and other legal obligations, or to respond to a lawful request from a court or public authority.

### To protect vital interests

In a genuine emergency, particularly during an in-person programme or homestay, we may use or disclose necessary information to protect a student's or another person's life, health, or safety.

We do not use personal data to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. If that changes, we will explain the decision, the logic involved, and the available safeguards before the processing begins.

## 4. When information is required

Some information is necessary to enter into or perform a contract, process payment, verify a booking, or meet a legal requirement. Required fields will normally be identified. If you do not provide required information, we may be unable to create an account, accept a booking, process a payment, or deliver the requested service.

Information requested for marketing, optional cookies, testimonials, or other optional activities is voluntary. Refusing it will not prevent you from buying a class or contacting us.

## 5. How we share personal data

We do not sell personal data.

We may share only the information reasonably necessary with:

- teachers and authorised Vida Verde staff;
- vetted host families and, where relevant, transport, accommodation, cultural-activity, or programme partners;
- payment processors and financial institutions;
- booking, calendar, video-meeting, email, customer-support, hosting, cloud-storage, analytics, mapping, security, and IT providers;
- professional advisers, such as accountants, auditors, insurers, and legal counsel;
- a parent, guardian, organisation, or travel coordinator who made or manages an authorised booking;
- courts, regulators, law-enforcement bodies, tax authorities, or other public authorities when disclosure is legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety; and
- a buyer, successor, or adviser involved in a genuine merger, restructuring, financing, or transfer of all or part of our organisation, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection safeguards.

Service providers may use personal data only to provide contracted services to us, comply with law, and protect their systems. Before launch, Vida Verde will maintain an up-to-date list of the principal providers used for payment, scheduling, video lessons, email marketing, analytics, hosting, and maps. You may request that list using the contact details in Section 15.

## 6. International transfers

Vida Verde is based in Ecuador and serves students internationally. Some teachers, students, host families, and service providers may be located in other countries, and some technology providers may store or access data outside Ecuador or outside your country.

Where an international transfer is regulated, we use a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards. Depending on the circumstances, these may include an adequacy decision, contractual protections, binding provider terms, explicit consent where legally valid, or a transfer necessary to perform a contract requested by you. You may contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

## 7. Payments

Payments are processed by an independent payment provider. Vida Verde receives transaction details such as your name, billing contact, amount, currency, status, and a payment reference, but complete card details do not pass through or remain on Vida Verde's servers.

The payment provider processes payment data under its own privacy policy and security obligations. The name of the active payment provider and a link to its privacy policy will be displayed at checkout.

## 8. Lessons, Google Meet, and communications

Online lessons are expected to be delivered through Google Meet. We use the information necessary to create and send the meeting link, identify participants, and support the lesson.

Lessons are not recorded by Vida Verde unless we explain why, identify who will have access, state how long the recording will be kept, and obtain any consent required by law. Students, parents, guardians, and teachers must not record or distribute a lesson without the prior permission of all affected participants and any legally required consent.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, Google, Meta, or another third-party service, that provider also processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use:

- **strictly necessary technologies** for security, login, checkout, consent records, and core website functions;
- **preference technologies** to remember language and display choices;
- **analytics technologies** to understand visits, bookings, lead-form submissions, downloads, and website performance; and
- **marketing technologies**, if introduced, to measure campaigns or show relevant advertising.

Where the law requires consent, analytics and marketing technologies remain disabled until you opt in. You can accept, reject, or change non-essential cookie choices through the website's cookie controls. You can also use browser settings to remove or block cookies, although blocking necessary technologies may prevent parts of the site from working.

The website may use Google Analytics 4 to measure aggregate site activity and conversion events. Vida Verde will configure analytics to minimise data collection and will disclose the active cookies, providers, purposes, and durations in the site's cookie preferences before launch.

## 10. Email marketing

We send promotional or nurture emails only when we have an appropriate legal basis, including consent where required. Downloading a free guide does not remove your right to choose whether to receive further marketing.

Every marketing email will include a one-click unsubscribe option. You may also unsubscribe by contacting us. We may still send non-marketing messages needed to manage an account, booking, payment, class, programme, safety issue, or legal notice.

## 11. Children and younger students

Vida Verde may teach younger students, but a person who is not legally able to enter into the relevant agreement must use our services only with the involvement and authorisation of a parent or legal guardian.

The parent or guardian must make or approve the booking, payment, account, and any consent required for the student's data. We collect only the information reasonably needed to teach the student, communicate safely, and provide the service. We do not knowingly use a child's data for behavioural advertising.

If you believe a child provided personal data without the required authorisation, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## 12. Your privacy rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, you may have the right to:

- be informed about how your personal data is used;
- access your personal data and receive a copy;
- correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
- request deletion when data is no longer needed or is being processed unlawfully;
- request suspension or restriction of processing;
- object to particular processing, including direct marketing;
- receive certain data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and request portability where applicable;
- withdraw consent at any time;
- object to or request human review of certain automated decisions;
- make a query or complaint about our processing; and
- lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority.

To exercise a right, contact us using Section 15 and state the right you wish to exercise. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify your identity and protect your data. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. Rights may be limited where an exception applies, including where retention is required by law or necessary for legal claims.

For Ecuador, the supervisory authority is the **Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales (SPDP)** at [https://spdp.gob.ec/](https://spdp.gob.ec/). If another country's data-protection law applies to you, you may also have the right to contact the supervisory authority in that country.

## 13. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, including to provide services and satisfy legal, accounting, tax, safeguarding, security, and dispute-resolution requirements.

Retention is determined by:

- the duration of your enquiry, account, course, package, programme, or homestay;
- the package-validity period displayed when you purchase;
- the time reasonably needed to provide follow-up support and maintain an accurate learning record;
- whether you remain subscribed to marketing;
- statutory retention periods for invoices, payments, contracts, and accounting records;
- the sensitivity of the information and risk of harm from unauthorised use; and
- any actual or anticipated complaint, incident, or legal claim.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it, anonymise it, or securely block it while deletion is pending or while a limited legal retention requirement applies. Backup copies are removed through our normal backup lifecycle.

## 14. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Measures may include access controls, least-privilege permissions, secure authentication, encryption in transit, backups, monitoring, provider due diligence, and staff confidentiality obligations.

No internet service is completely secure. Please use a strong, unique password and tell us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access to your account or communications.
